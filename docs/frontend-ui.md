# Frontend UI

Shared tokens live in `frontend/src/app/globals.css`. Wrappers live in `frontend/src/components/ui/`.

- `.btn-primary` — black fill
- `.btn-accent` — blue fill, used for Add to cart and the Checkout button on the “Added to cart” note (`helpKey` `notice.checkout`)
- `.btn-secondary` — black border
- `.btn-danger` — red border
- `.input-field` / `.select-field`
- `.card-panel` / `.table-wrap` / `.empty-state` / `.spinner`
- The product list search box (`admin.product.search`) and collection menu (`admin.product.filter_collection`) sit to the left of Add product.
- Admin product headings (`admin.product.sort_title`, `admin.product.sort_price`, `admin.product.sort_variants`) sort that table. An arrow marks the active column.
- `.select-field` — product option dropdowns on the product page and the add-to-cart dialog
- Brand mint: `#7A9E8F` (`brand` in Tailwind)

Unknown shop addresses use `frontend/src/app/not-found.tsx` inside the normal header and footer. The page shows the circular mark, 404, and “Page not found”, then Home (`nav.home`) and Collections (`nav.shop`). `/checkout/success` and `/checkout/cancel` are real pages, so they do not use this screen.

`Button` requires `helpKey`. Tips are loaded from `/api/help-tips` and stored in `help_tips`. `HelpButton` / `HelpLink` apply `title` only after mount so the tooltip does not mismatch SSR HTML. When you add a control, add the `helpKey` and a row in `backend-server/src/seed/defaults.ts` (upserted on boot).

The storefront is English. Marketing sentences (announcement, hero, story, shop, newsletter) come from `site_settings`. The announcement is `slogan` and sits on the gray bar under the header. The hero photo is `frontend/public/uploads/site/hero.jpg`. The header logo is the rectangular LUUX wordmark at `/images/luux-logo.jpg` (`site_settings.logo`), shown at 35px tall like luuxmart.com. The browser tab icon stays the circular mark at `/images/luux-mark.jpg` (`site_settings.logo_icon`). The footer uses that same circle. Under Terms and Policies, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and Pinterest are round icons when those profile links are saved in Settings (`facebook_url`, `instagram_url`, `threads_url`, `pinterest_url`). Their tips are `footer.facebook`, `footer.instagram`, `footer.threads`, and `footer.pinterest`. The browser tab uses the site SEO title (`LUUX`) on most pages. On a product page it is the product title plus the company name, for example `Oboe - Pendant Lamp – LUUX`. Search, account, and cart use `/images/icon-search.svg`, `/images/icon-account.svg`, and `/images/icon-cart.svg`. Header search opens `/search?q=`. That page keeps the same field on the page, filled with the current words, and Search (`search.submit`) runs the query again. `GET /api/search` matches that text against the product title, handle, description, tags, and type. The match ignores letter case, so `lapu` finds Laputa. `%` and `_` in the query are treated as ordinary characters. On `/account`, Google (`account.google`) and Facebook (`account.facebook`) sit above Sign in. A staff email and password on that form opens `/admin`. The link under Sign in reads Sign up (`helpKey` `account.signup`) and opens the create-account form, which asks for the password twice. On that form the link reads Already have an account? (`account.have_account`). Find an order (`account.track`) sits under the sign-in form and asks for the order number and checkout email. A signed-in account shows Personal details (`account.save`): name, contact number, invoice address, and shipping address. Shipping address is the same as the invoice address (`account.same_address`) keeps one address. Each order row (`account.order.open`) opens `/account/orders/[number]` with the lamps, addresses, totals, status, and tracking. Track shipment (`account.tracking`) opens the carrier link when staff saved one.

The cart lists each lamp, then the subtotal, the shipping rate, and the total. Rates come from `shipping_rates` through `GET /api/shipping`. Staff add and delete those rates on `/admin/shipping`. A rate whose countries are `*` is offered for every country. A rate limited to codes such as `HK` is offered only for those countries. The chosen rate is stored in `localStorage` as `luux_shipping` and checkout opens on that same rate. Hong Kong is the default when nothing has been chosen. Checkout then asks for an email, a contact number, and an invoice address before Pay with Stripe (`checkout.pay`). The invoice address has a first name, last name, optional company, address, optional apartment, city, optional region, optional postal code, and country. The shipping address is that invoice address unless the customer unchecks Shipping address is the same as the invoice address (`checkout.same_address`) and enters a delivery address. The rate list follows the delivery country. A contact number needs at least 6 digits. The API stores both addresses on the order and will not open Stripe without them. A signed-in customer’s saved details fill the empty fields, and a signed-in payment writes those details back to the account. The thank-you page links a signed-in customer to the order (`checkout.view_order`). A guest can look the order up there with the order number and email.

A cookie bar sits at the bottom of every shop page, including checkout. It is hidden on `/admin`. The sentence comes from `site_settings.cookie_notice` (Settings in the staff area). Allow (`cookies.allow`) stores `all`. Reject (`cookies.reject`) and Save in Cookie settings (`cookies.save`) store `necessary`. Cookie settings (`cookies.settings`) shows Strictly necessary locked on (`cookies.necessary`). Cookie policy (`cookies.policy`) opens `/policies/cookie-policy`. The login cookie and the cart, shipping, and currency stored in the browser stay available either way. The shop does not set advertising cookies. The choice is saved in `cookie_consents` as a SHA-256 of `ADMIN_SESSION_SECRET` and the visitor IP (`X-Real-IP` when nginx sends it). The address itself is not stored. The same IP is not asked again. A shared office or mobile network shares one choice. Local `npm run dev` has no nginx, so every browser on the machine shares `127.0.0.1`.

The header control labelled `{code} / EN` (`helpKey` `nav.currency`) changes the display currency. Choices and rates (foreign units per 1 HKD) come from the `currencies` table, seeded in `backend-server/src/seed/defaults.ts` and served by `GET /api/currencies`. The choice is stored in `localStorage` as `luux_currency` and defaults to HKD. Collection, product, cart, and checkout prices use that rate. Stripe still charges HKD; checkout says so when another currency is selected. Order history stays in HKD.

The header no longer links to About Us. `/pages/about-us` redirects to `/pages/contact`. That page is two equal columns: the About Us page (title and `body_html` from the `pages` row `about-us`) on the left, and the Contact Us page plus the message form on the right. The form stores each note for staff on Messages. When outgoing mail is saved in Settings, a copy is also sent to the contact email. The homepage “Learn more” beside the story also opens `/pages/contact`. About Us opens with a highlighted note (`.about-note`): all products on this website are replicas, not original products. Boot adds that paragraph when the `about-us` body does not already contain it, and replaces the earlier wording that talked about problems.

The desktop COLLECTIONS link (`helpKey` `nav.shop`) opens `/collections`. Hovering it lists All (`nav.shop_all`, `/collections/all`), then each collection. `/collections/all` lists every published lamp, in title order, with the same product cards as a collection page. It is not a row in `collections`. Below the `md` breakpoint the header hides the text links and shows a menu button (`helpKey` `nav.menu`) on the left. It opens Home, Collections, All, each collection, and Contact Us. The collections index heading is Collections. A collection page shows COLLECTIONS above the collection title. The storefront header, footer, shop index, featured products, collection grids, search, and product page use the browser width with a 40px side inset (`px-6 md:px-10`). The shop index is three collection photos across. Featured products are four across on a wide screen. Collection and search results are six across once the window is at least 1536px wide, so each card stays compact. Every product card (`ProductCard`) is a 4:5 photo with 10px corners, then the title, price, and an Add to cart button. That button opens `AddToCartDialog`: a wide panel with a large product photo beside the title, price, and one dropdown per option. Choosing a dropdown value and Add to cart puts that variant in the cart and shows the checkout note. Close uses `dialog.close`. The photo still opens the product page. Narrow screens show two columns, and the dialog stacks the photo above the details.

Product photos live in `ProductGallery`. The main photo is three-quarters of the product-page column. The gallery stays inside the viewport: the main photo is a square, and the thumbnail strip scrolls inside itself. The page always opens on the main photo, which staff choose in the product editor. That photo is stored at gallery position 1 and is also the photo on product cards. Thumbnail clicks change the main photo. Size, colour, and other options are dropdowns (`OptionSelects`). Changing a dropdown then jumps to that variant’s assigned photo when it has one. Staff assign those photos in Edit photo with AI by ticking option values. A value already used by another photo is greyed out there. Prev/next arrows cycle the gallery (`product.gallery.prev`, `product.gallery.next`, `product.gallery.thumb`). HTML inside product and page copy (`.prose-luux`) wraps, and its images cannot grow wider than the column.
